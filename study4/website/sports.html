Why MLB outcomes?

Sports outcomes are structurally different from financial markets: discrete events with bounded outcomes, no random-walk dynamics, and ground truth available within hours of game completion. If the same calibration patterns appear across both domains, the effect is unlikely to be a quirk of any single prediction context.

The five quantities span different types of knowledge: total runs (sportsbook-priced, skill-driven), total strikeouts (pitching-specific), total home runs (discrete, low-count, skewed), game duration (pace/replays, not a baseball-skill quantity), and attendance (multi-factor: team popularity, day of week, venue capacity, weather).
