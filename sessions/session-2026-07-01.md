## Decisions

- **Nav label**: "Sports Outcomes" (parallels "Daily Forecast", "Horizon Backtest", "Cross-Domain" — content-descriptive, not "Study 5")
- **Normalization for non-financial quantities**: Use per-quantity mean(actual) as the denominator in MAD/MEAD instead of per-row actual. Fixes the `abs_dev / 0` blow-up that would otherwise arise from total_home_runs when actuals=0. μ = MEAD/MAD is invariant to the choice, so headline calibration numbers are unchanged; accuracy becomes "typical error as fraction of typical value" which is more interpretable across the 5 quantities.
- **Structure**: Mirror Cross-Domain page exactly (filter → cards → chart → table → coverage → explainer), not Daily Forecast or Backtest — because Study 5 is a single-snapshot design like Cross-Domain, not longitudinal or multi-horizon
- **Coverage section**: List the 4 postponed games explicitly in a table, not just a count. Reviewer-friendly and honest about missing data
